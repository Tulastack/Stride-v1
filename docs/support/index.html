Recording tips

Film from the side, at hip height, with the whole body in frame from start to finish. Keep the phone still and the light good. Stride uses your phone's motion sensors to find true vertical, so a tripod or a steady hand helps.

Deleting your account

Open Settings in the app and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your videos, analyses and coaching history. You can also email us to ask for deletion.
